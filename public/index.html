Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Praveka Life Science (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use this website, place an order or contact us.

1. Information we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, phone number, the product and quantity you ask about.
  • Orders: your name, mobile number, delivery address, PIN code, the items you order and the online payment transaction details needed to confirm payment.
  • Reviews: your name, mobile number, rating and review text. We use the mobile number for review integrity and do not display it publicly.
  • Mobile number updates: your mobile number, if you subscribe to farming tips and offers.
  • On your device: your shopping cart is saved in your browser’s storage so it is not lost when you leave the page. It stays on your device.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the website working.

2. How we use your information

  • To confirm, pack, deliver and support your orders.
  • To call or message you back about your enquiry.
  • To send farming tips and offers, only if you subscribed. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To keep records we must keep under tax, accounting and pesticide-trade laws.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of the website.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with people who help us serve you, and only what is needed: delivery and courier partners, our online payment provider (Razorpay) for payment processing and verification, website hosting and IT service providers, and government authorities when the law requires it.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No website or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. We keep your information for as long as needed to complete your order, answer your questions, and meet legal and tax record-keeping requirements.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website uses browser storage only to remember your cart. We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you offers. Write to us at the email below or message us on WhatsApp. We will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to keep some records where the law requires.

7. Children

Our products are for adult farmers and growers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Links to other services

Links such as WhatsApp or Google Maps open services run by other companies, which have their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy
